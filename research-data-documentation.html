02 / Data Provenance

Source of Learning Records

AURA EDU is a browser-based adaptive English learning research prototype. Its grammar modules can create local learning session records.

The prototype currently uses localStorage for learner profiles and module records. These records are not stored in a centrally authenticated research database.

Grammar Modules

G1 — Verb to Be (am / is / are)

G2 — Subject–Verb Agreement

G3 — Sentence Construction

The existence of local records does not establish that they were collected from recruited research participants. Prototype test records and simulated demonstrations must be distinguished from future empirical research data.

04 / Learner Identity Binding

Local Identity Association

AURA EDU uses a Learner Identity Service to associate a learning session with the active local learner.

At session creation, the system records the learner identifier and module. Before saving a completed session, identity consistency checks can prevent accidental reassignment.

The service is not a user authentication mechanism.

Learner identity association does not verify a person's real-world identity. Records without reliable identity associations must not be automatically assigned to another learner.

05 / Historical Compatibility

Legacy Learning Records

AURA EDU retains historical records created under earlier versions of the learning modules.

Historical compatibility checks distinguish records that satisfy current identity requirements from records requiring further review.

A REVIEW classification is not equivalent to deleting or invalidating a record.

Previous development audit: 13 historical records were inspected, with 4 classified PASS and 9 REVIEW. This is a documented prior test result, not a live recalculation.

Reproducibility Considerations

Future research documentation should preserve data schema versions, decision rules, module versions, quality classifications and reproducible analysis procedures.

JSON backups can support local inspection and archival review, but backup existence alone does not guarantee reproducibility.

07 / Research Ethics and Limitations

Research Ethics

AURA EDU currently functions as a research and development prototype.

Future research involving children should follow applicable institutional ethical approval, parental permission, child assent, privacy protection and appropriate data management procedures.

Current Limitations

The current prototype does not establish educational effectiveness, validated mastery thresholds or verified real-world learner identity.

Historical data quality limitations must be reported transparently.
