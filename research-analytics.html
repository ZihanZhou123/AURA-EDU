    RESEARCH TRANSPARENCY

    Methodological Limitations

    This dashboard demonstrates the structure of a proposed learning analytics system. It is not a report of completed student research.

    Four items per knowledge point and assessment stage provide only limited evidence. Formal mastery claims would require a validated assessment design, additional observations and appropriate reliability analysis.

    The seven-day delayed reassessment interval is a proposed design choice rather than an empirically established optimal interval.
