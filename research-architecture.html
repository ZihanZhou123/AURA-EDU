Research Architecture & Proposed Contributions

AURA EDU explores how structured instructional scaffolding, explainable adaptive learning decisions and learning analytics can be integrated into an English grammar learning prototype for students aged 10–12.

Explore System Architecture Proposed Research Contributions
01 / Research Foundation

From Learner Differences to Adaptive Support

Students may experience different difficulties while learning the same English grammar concept. AURA EDU investigates a design approach in which learning evidence informs differentiated support and provisional learning recommendations.

Educational Problem

Individual Learning Differences

Learners vary in their initial understanding, grammar difficulties and support needs.

Design Response

Adaptive Instructional Scaffolding

Graduated hints and targeted feedback respond to observable task difficulties.

Research Focus

Independent Application

Immediate independent reassessment provides evidence beyond supported task completion.

Central Research Question

How can learning analytics-informed adaptive instructional scaffolding be designed to support and examine independent English grammar learning among primary school students aged 10–12?

02 / System Architecture

A Dual-Layer Adaptive Learning Framework

The framework connects learning assessment, instructional support, provisional decisions and descriptive learning analytics.

01 · Learner Diagnostic Assessment Initial grammar responses and performance evidence
↓
Layer A · Content Adaptation Knowledge-level tasks and recommended learning paths
Layer B · Support Adaptation Graduated hints, feedback and error-specific guidance
↓
02 · Scaffolded Practice First-attempt correctness, hint exposure and error codes
↓
03 · Immediate Independent Reassessment Independent performance following instructional support
↓
04 · Explainable Learning Decision Rule-based provisional recommendations informed by recorded evidence
↓
05 · Learning Analytics & Process Reports Session-level and question-level descriptive evidence

03 / Adaptive Mechanisms

Making Instructional Decisions Explainable

Support Layer

Graduated Scaffolding

Students receive different levels of instructional support when encountering difficulties. The system records the highest hint level used.

Knowledge Layer

Learning Path Recommendations

Assessment and practice evidence contribute to provisional decisions about subsequent learning activities.

Assessment

Independent Reassessment

Students complete an immediate reassessment stage to examine performance without the same instructional hints.

Explainability

Transparent Decision Rules

The prototype uses structured, inspectable rules rather than claiming an independently validated autonomous AI decision system.

04 / Learning Analytics Evidence

What Does AURA EDU Record?

The research prototype connects learning activities with structured evidence suitable for descriptive analysis.

Evidence Recorded Indicator Research Purpose
Diagnostic Initial assessment percentage Describe initial performance
First Attempt First-attempt correctness Examine task performance before further support
Hint Exposure Highest recorded hint level Describe instructional support use
Error Evidence Recorded error codes Identify observable error patterns
Practice Practice assessment percentage Describe supported task outcomes
Immediate Reassessment Reassessment percentage Describe immediate independent performance
Learning Decision Mastery state and recommended path Inspect provisional rule-based recommendations
Learner Attribution Local learner ID and session ID Separate matched and legacy records
Question-Level Analytics Learner Process Report Cross-Module Evidence

Interpretation Boundary: Recorded hint use does not establish psychological dependence on hints. Immediate reassessment is not equivalent to delayed retention. Descriptive comparisons do not demonstrate causal effectiveness.

05 / Proposed Research Contributions

Connecting Educational Theory and Technology Design

The following represent proposed areas of doctoral research contribution, not empirically established findings.

CONTRIBUTION 01

Educational Design

Operationalise graduated instructional scaffolding and formative assessment within structured English grammar tasks.

Explore how learners may be guided toward independent application rather than receiving direct answers alone.

CONTRIBUTION 02

Explainable Adaptive Technology

Develop and examine a dual-layer adaptive design combining content recommendations with differentiated instructional support.

Make decision logic inspectable through explicit rules and supporting evidence.

CONTRIBUTION 03

Learning Analytics Methodology

Investigate how first-attempt performance, errors, support exposure and independent reassessment can be analysed together.

Inform future research designs evaluating learning processes, usability and knowledge retention.

07 / Doctoral Research Roadmap

From Functional Prototype to Research Study

PHASE 01

Expert Evaluation

Review the instructional framework, grammar content, adaptive rules, learning analytics measures and research instruments.

PHASE 02

Usability and Feasibility

Conduct appropriately approved pilot testing with target learners, examining usability, comprehension and feasibility.

PHASE 03

Empirical Investigation

Investigate learning outcomes, independent grammar application, support patterns and delayed knowledge retention.

Research Ethics: Future research involving children requires appropriate institutional approval, guardian consent, age-appropriate assent and responsible handling of learner data.
