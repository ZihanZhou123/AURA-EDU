01 / Research Problem

One Learning Path Does Not Fit Every Learner.

Students may differ in their prior knowledge, grammar difficulties and instructional support needs. Fixed learning sequences may not adequately respond to these differences.

AURA EDU investigates how learner responses, error patterns, hint usage and independent reassessment can inform instructional decisions.

Central Research Question

How can a learning analytics-informed adaptive system use learners' performance and instructional support data to guide English grammar learning activities for primary school students?

03 / Adaptive Framework

A Dual-Layer Adaptive Learning System

LAYER 01

Question-Level Adaptation

Graduated hints, feedback and structured error identification during individual tasks.

LAYER 02

Knowledge-Level Adaptation

Learning path recommendations informed by evidence collected across grammar tasks.

Evidence-Based Mastery

Distinguishing assisted task completion from independent application of knowledge.

Two-Stage Reassessment Design

Immediate reassessment is implemented to examine independent application. Delayed reassessment remains a proposed future feature for examining subsequent knowledge retention.

Research Interpretation and Limitations

Privacy and Research Ethics Learner names and identifiable records should not be published in a public PhD portfolio. Use anonymised or clearly simulated demonstration data.

Evidence Scope: The learner reports demonstrate the prototype's data processing and descriptive reporting capabilities. They do not establish that the system improves English grammar learning. Delayed independent reassessment remains a future development goal.
