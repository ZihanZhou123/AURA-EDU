Proposed Research Questions

RQ1 · How do students respond to different levels of instructional scaffolding?
RQ2 · How is scaffolded practice associated with independent spoken communication?
RQ3 · Which task-level indicators help identify learners' support needs?

Pedagogical and Technical Foundations

Zone of Proximal Development, instructional scaffolding, formative assessment, self-regulated learning and knowledge tracing are relevant theoretical and methodological directions. These are conceptual references, not claims of empirically demonstrated effectiveness.

Open-source architecture inspirations: Kolibri, LiveKit Agents, LanguageTool, and pyBKT. These projects are not integrated into this standalone prototype.

Research Integrity and Next Steps

The curriculum-themed examples are original and not verified FLTRP textbook units. The system is rule-driven, not a deployed large-language-model tutor. Formal textbook verification, ethics approval, consent and safeguarding, independent speaking assessment, teacher validation and longitudinal evaluation are necessary before making educational efficacy claims.
