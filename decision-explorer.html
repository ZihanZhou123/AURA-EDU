RESEARCH TRANSPARENCY

How This Prototype Makes Decisions

The Evidence-Based Mastery Model (EBMM) is a proposed research design, not an established or psychometrically validated assessment instrument.

This demonstration uses transparent, provisional rule-based thresholds to illustrate possible instructional decisions.

Foundation

Support learners showing substantial difficulty in independent reassessment.

Guided

Provide targeted practice when evidence suggests developing understanding.

Challenge

Provide more demanding tasks when immediate independent performance is strong.

Immediate reassessment alone cannot establish stable mastery. A delayed independent assessment and additional evidence are required before considering demonstrated mastery.
