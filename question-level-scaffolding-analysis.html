09 / RESEARCH INTEGRITY

Evidence Boundaries

What Can Be Observed?

Recorded attempts, first-attempt correctness, final completion, hint levels, error codes, and immediate reassessment, when available.

What Remains Unverified?

Exact hint-click chronology, causal benefit, genuine hint dependency, long-term retention and generalisable educational effectiveness.
