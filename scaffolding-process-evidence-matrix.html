06 / RESEARCH INTEGRITY

What This Matrix Can and Cannot Show

Supported Observations

Session-level descriptive comparisons of recorded practice attempts, hint levels, error entries and immediate independent reassessment results.

Unverified Conclusions

Actual hint dependency, causal effects, durable mastery, independent learner identity and participant authenticity are not established by this matrix.

Data provenance: Unless a record contains an explicit recognised provenance field, its source is classified as unverified. A self-reported label is not independently authenticated.
