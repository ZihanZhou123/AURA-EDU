01 / Research Vision

Why Adaptive Learning?

Learners differ in prior knowledge, learning difficulties and support needs. A learning system should respond to these differences rather than assume that one sequence is appropriate for everyone.

RESEARCH QUESTION

Adaptive Support

How can learning evidence inform differentiated instructional support?

RESEARCH QUESTION

Independent Learning

How can a system distinguish supported task completion from independent performance?

RESEARCH QUESTION

Learning Analytics

How can question-level evidence make adaptive learning decisions more transparent and explainable?

02 / Research Motivation

Inspired by Individual Differences in Learning.

Teaching Experience

My experience providing one-to-one tutoring highlighted that learners may require different explanations, levels of guidance and opportunities for practice, even when studying the same concept.

This experience motivated my interest in designing adaptive support that is not limited to individual tutoring settings.

From Business Analytics to Learning Analytics

My academic background in Business Analytics motivated me to explore how structured data, transparent decision rules and analytical reasoning could be applied to educational learning processes.

AURA EDU connects this analytical perspective with educational questions about feedback, scaffolding and independent learning.

03 / System Architecture

How AURA EDU Connects Learning and Evidence

A dual-layer adaptive framework combines question-level instructional support with knowledge-level learning recommendations.

01 · Diagnostic Assessment

Collect initial grammar performance and identify task difficulties.

02 · Question-Level Scaffolding

Provide graduated hints and feedback during learning tasks.

03 · Practice Evidence

Record first-attempt correctness, hint exposure and error codes.

↓
04 · Independent Reassessment

Examine immediate independent application after practice.

05 · Adaptive Decision Rules

Generate provisional learning recommendations from recorded evidence.

06 · Learning Analytics

Present learner-level and question-level descriptive evidence.

04 / Proposed Research Contributions

Connecting Educational Theory, Adaptive Technology and Learning Analytics

AURA EDU provides a functional design foundation for investigating three proposed areas of doctoral research contribution. These are research directions, not empirically established findings.

CONTRIBUTION 01

Educational Design

Explore how graduated instructional scaffolding and formative assessment can support English grammar learning.

Emphasise independent application rather than answer provision alone.

CONTRIBUTION 02

Explainable Adaptive Technology

Investigate a dual-layer adaptive framework combining learning content recommendations with differentiated instructional support.

Use inspectable decision rules and recorded learning evidence.

CONTRIBUTION 03

Learning Analytics Methodology

Examine how first-attempt correctness, error patterns, hint exposure and independent reassessment can be analysed together.

Inform future educational evaluation and research design.
