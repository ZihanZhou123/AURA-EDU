03 / METRIC DEFINITIONS

Learning Analytics Measurement Dictionary

Indicator Operational Definition Interpretation Boundary
Diagnostic Performance Stored diagnostic percentage. Initial performance within the recorded assessment.
First-Attempt Accuracy Correct first attempts / practice questions with known first-attempt status. Not equivalent to final practice accuracy.
Revision Frequency Questions with more than one recorded attempt / questions with attempt arrays. Multiple attempts do not independently prove reflection.
Hint Exposure Questions with highestHintUsed above zero / questions with recognised hint values. Recorded hint level is not a complete hint-click event log.
Error Entries Number of recorded errorCodes entries. Not a count of unique misconceptions.
Immediate Independent Reassessment Stored reassessment percentage. Immediate performance does not verify durable mastery.
Mastery State Stored rule-based classification. Not an independently validated learning outcome.
04 / PROVENANCE

Development Data vs Research Participant Data

Current Prototype Evidence

Local development and testing records demonstrate that the prototype can collect and analyse learning process information.

These records must not automatically be presented as real participant findings.

Future Empirical Evidence

A formal study requires documented recruitment, appropriate consent and ethics approval, verified participant provenance, a research protocol and appropriate outcome measurement.

Historical records without verified provenance remain unverified. A stored label alone does not authenticate a participant or establish consent.

06 / INTERPRETATION FRAMEWORK

Evidence and Research Claims

Supported by Prototype Logs

The system can record selected learning events and generate descriptive analyses of practice, hints, errors and immediate reassessment when the relevant fields exist.

Descriptive Evidence

Not Yet Established

The logs do not demonstrate that scaffolding caused learning gains, that students became independent, or that improvements persisted over time.

Requires Empirical Validation
07 / FUTURE STUDY DESIGN

Research Validation Roadmap

Research Questions

How do learners with different initial grammar difficulties interact with differentiated scaffolding?

How are hint exposure, revision patterns and independent assessment performance associated?

Evaluation Requirements

Establish appropriate recruitment, ethics and consent procedures, baseline assessment, validated outcomes, independent and delayed reassessment, and a justified analysis plan.

08 / FINAL REVIEW

Research Analytics Completion Boundary

Prototype Readiness Statement

The AURA EDU research analytics prototype provides an inspectable framework for learning-process recording, adaptive decision explanation and descriptive evidence review.

Functional testing can establish whether these prototype components work as intended. It does not establish educational effectiveness.

After technical verification, development should focus on end-to-end integration, usability, privacy safeguards and PhD portfolio presentation rather than adding further standalone analytics pages.
