01 / Research Approach

Design and Development Research

Proposed Research Approach

Research Design

AURA EDU is being developed as an educational technology research prototype. A Design and Development Research approach is proposed to guide the systematic design, development and evaluation of its adaptive learning mechanisms.

本项目拟采用设计与开发研究方法， 系统地设计、开发并评估自适应英语 语法学习原型。

Current activities focus on prototype implementation and functional verification. Future stages may include expert evaluation, usability testing and empirical investigation of student learning outcomes.

01 / Analysis Learner needs, grammar difficulties and instructional requirements
02 / Design Scaffolding, assessment and adaptive decision rules
03 / Development G1/G2/G3 learning modules and analytics components
04 / Evaluation Functional testing and planned empirical validation

Methodological distinction: A working prototype is not equivalent to an empirically validated educational intervention. Future studies require appropriate research design and ethics.

02 / Research Questions

Proposed Research Questions

RQ1 — Design

How can learner performance, error patterns and instructional support needs inform the design of an adaptive English grammar learning system?

如何利用学生表现、错误模式与 教学支持需求设计自适应英语 语法学习系统？

RQ2 — Adaptive Decisions

How can question-level and knowledge-level evidence inform explainable instructional scaffolding decisions?

如何利用题目层面与知识点层面的 学习证据，形成可解释的教学 支架决策？

RQ3 — Prototype Evaluation

To what extent does the prototype satisfy predefined functional, data quality, usability and explainability criteria?

原型在功能正确性、数据质量、 可用性和决策可解释性方面， 能否达到预先制定的评价标准？

Future Empirical Research

RQ4 — Learning Outcomes

How does adaptive scaffolding relate to independent grammar application and knowledge retention among learners aged 10–12?

对于10–12岁学习者， 自适应教学支架与独立语法应用 及知识保持之间存在怎样的关系？

03 / Adaptive Learning Mechanism

Dual-Layer Adaptive Framework

Layer 1 — Question-Level

The proposed system provides graduated hints, targeted feedback and opportunities for self-correction during grammar tasks.

第一层：题目层面的提示、 错误反馈和学生自主修改。

Layer 2 — Knowledge-Level

Learning evidence across tasks informs provisional recommendations for foundation, guided practice or more advanced challenges.

第二层：根据跨题目学习证据， 推荐基础巩固、引导练习 或进阶挑战。

Evidence-Based Mastery

Assisted completion should be distinguished from independent performance. Mastery thresholds require future validation.

区分借助提示完成任务与 独立掌握知识，避免把 提示后的正确答案直接 等同于知识掌握。

Two-Stage Reassessment

Immediate independent reassessment examines application after support. Delayed reassessment is proposed to examine subsequent retention.

两阶段复测： 即时独立复测与计划中的 延迟知识保持评估。

04 / Prototype Evaluation

Evaluation Framework

Prototype evaluation should use predefined criteria and distinguish functional test evidence from educational effectiveness evidence.

Dimension Evaluation Focus Evidence / Method Status
Functional Correctness Navigation, module operation and interaction correctness Manual test cases, pass/fail records Testing underway
Data Integrity Preservation of historical learning records Local inventory, JSON backup comparison Previously tested
Identity Consistency Local learner-session association Identity binding checks, legacy classification 4 PASS / 9 REVIEW in prior audit
Explainability Clarity and consistency of adaptive recommendations Decision rule inspection, expert review Further review planned
Usability Task completion, navigation and learner experience Observational usability testing and feedback Planned
Instructional Quality Hint relevance, feedback and grammar alignment Curriculum mapping, educator expert review Planned
Learning Effectiveness Independent performance and retention Future empirical study Not established

Evaluation rule: Each future test should record its objective, procedure, expected result, observed result and pass/fail decision. Passing a software test must not be reported as evidence of improved student learning.

06 / Future Empirical Research

Proposed Empirical Evaluation Plan

Planned

Participants

Learners aged 10–12, with recruitment criteria and sample size determined during research planning.

研究对象：10–12岁学生。 具体样本量与招募标准 尚需研究设计确定。

Planned

Learning Content

English grammar and sentence construction, with curriculum alignment and assessment content reviewed before use.

学习内容：英语语法与句子构建， 后续依据中国课程与所选教材 完成正式知识点映射。

Planned

Learning Measures

Initial independent performance, hint usage, revision behaviour, immediate reassessment and delayed retention measures.

评价指标：独立作答表现、 提示使用、自主修改、 即时复测及延迟保持。

Planned

Study Design

A suitable comparative or repeated-measures design may be considered. The final design and analysis plan require further development.

可考虑比较研究或重复测量设计， 但尚未确定实验分组、 测量时间点及统计分析方案。

Ethical Requirements

  • Institutional ethical approval before participant research
  • Appropriate parental or guardian permission
  • Age-appropriate child assent
  • Data minimisation and privacy protection
  • Clear distinction between prototype tests and empirical data
